\program{Funkcje i wykresy}
\label{prog:F05}

\programstub{%
\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia --- patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

Argument: a function is a machine with one output per input, and shifting,
scaling and reflecting its graph are four moves you can do by eye. Payoff:
an activation function is a graph you can recognise; weight and bias are
scale and shift; a monotone transformation does not move the argmax, which
is why temperature changes what you sample but not what is most likely.

\medskip
\textbf{Planowana długość:} 45 ramek.
