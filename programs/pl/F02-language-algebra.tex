\label{prog:F02}

\programstub{%
\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia \dash{} patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

Argument: a symbol stands for a quantity you have not fixed yet, and
rearranging is a sequence of legal moves. Payoff: read a paper's equation as
executable code; rearrange a loss so the thing you want is on the left.
Trap: $(a+b)^{2}$ and the reader who writes $a^{2}+b^{2}$, elicited
before it sets.

\medskip
\textbf{Planowana długość:} 50 ramek.
}
