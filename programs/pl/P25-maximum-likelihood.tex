\program{Estymacja i metoda największej wiarogodności}
\label{prog:P25}

\programstub{%
\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia \dash{} patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

Argument: an estimator is a function of the sample; bias and variance are
two different ways of being wrong; maximum likelihood picks the parameter
that makes the observed data least surprising. Payoff: training a language
model is maximum likelihood, and the cross-entropy loss is the negative log-
likelihood of the observed tokens \dash{} one derivation that reframes the
entire training objective, and from which label smoothing, class weighting
and the \code{n-1} in a sample variance all fall out. MAP as maximum
likelihood with a prior, which is exactly weight decay with a Gaussian prior
\dash{} the cleanest available statement of what regularisation *is*.

\medskip
\textbf{Planowana długość:} 55 ramek.
