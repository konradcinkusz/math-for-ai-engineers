\textit{[Opis do przetłumaczenia \dash{} patrz wydanie angielskie.]}

The loss as maximum likelihood [P25]; its gradient as \code{p - y} [P17];
the optimiser and its bias correction [P19]; precision and loss scaling
[P1]; the schedule and the curvature bound [P16]; gradient clipping and
minibatch noise [P20]. Then the diagnostic half: a loss curve read as
evidence \dash{} what a plateau, a spike, a divergence and a suspiciously smooth
descent each imply, and which of them are distinguishable from noise [P26].

uncertainty, plotted on log-log axes [F3], with the fit's extrapolation
error stated \dash{} not as a law of nature.
