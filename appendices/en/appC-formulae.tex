\chapter{Formula Reference}
\label{app:C}

\noindent
This appendix exists to fix the one criticism of the programmed-learning
method that is entirely fair: a book of frames is an excellent teacher and a
useless reference. The frame numbers in every Summary already point backwards;
this collects them, so that a reader who needs one identity six months from now
does not have to remember which program taught it.
