\chapter{Notation and Symbols}
\label{app:B}

\programstub{%
Every symbol the book uses, in the order a reader meets it, with the frame
that introduces it. This is the appendix the front matter's \emph{How to read
a formula} points back to, and it exists because a reader who has never seen
$\sum$ must not be stopped by it in Program~F4.

It also carries the notation decisions this book has taken where usage is
split: that $\log$ means the natural logarithm throughout; that vectors are
columns; the numerator layout convention for matrix derivatives, and the
transposes that follow from it; and that the Polish edition writes $\Var$ and
$\Ex$ rather than the $D^2$ and $M$ of the older Polish textbooks.
}
