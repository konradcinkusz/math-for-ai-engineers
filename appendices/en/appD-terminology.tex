%% appD-terminology --- Polish--English Terminology

\chapter{Polish--English Terminology}
\label{app:D}

\programstub{%
The terms this book uses, in both languages, with the cases where Polish usage
has not settled marked as unsettled rather than quietly decided.

Follows the precedent of the companion volume's glossary. The rule is the same
one: where an English term has no established Polish form, the English term is
kept, italicised, with a Polish gloss on first use --- because inventing a
Polish word that no Polish engineer says is worse than borrowing one.
}
